# Frontend handoff — state at 2026-11-14 (end of session 2)

## Tour (live click-through demo)
A slide-style walk through the LIVE app for a ~5 min demo: 9 beats ("A Monday in the life of the campus energy manager"), no timers.
- **Keys**: `T` (or the *Tour* button in the header) start · `→` / Space / Next button advance · `←` back · `1`-`9` jump to a beat · `N` speaker notes · `Esc` exit (restores theme, layers, week, model and the real campus). Keys are ignored while typing in an input.
- **Code**: `src/tour/steps.ts` (data: the 9 beats), `facts.ts` (live numbers for captions), `actions.ts` (absolute state helpers), `useTour.ts` (zustand: idx, sub, next/prev/goto), `TourOverlay.tsx` (card, spotlight, covers, keys; mounted in the router root in `routes.tsx` so it survives `/catalogue`). UI that lives in local React state is driven through `src/store/useUi.ts` (sidebar, Settings, assistant question, header drop-downs, timeline zoom).
- **Add / edit a step**: add a `TourStep` to `STEPS`: `enter()` sets the starting state, `clicks[]` are micro-reveals (button label "Next: <label>", `action` = the "Now: ..." line, `spot` = CSS selector for the spotlight ring; `data-tour` attributes exist on time pill, SIMULATED pill, calendar, model menu, timeline, bell, chat). Text may be a function of `TF` (never type numbers by hand). `cover(sub)` shows a full-screen slide (`json`, `insights`, `facts`).
- **Determinism rule**: `enter()` and `clicks` must set ABSOLUTE state (never toggle). Every Back / jump runs `resetBase()` (real campus, standard model, Monday 11:00 of week 17, 2D, nothing open) + `enter()` + the first n clicks, instantly, animating only the last; steps are independent of each other. Plain Next on a micro-click just runs that click on top.
- The old pitch (`src/pitch/`) is not mounted by `App.tsx` any more (left untouched).

## Session 4 (Aiman, branch `aiman-demo-minimal`): monitor features on top of the redesign
- **Wider map**: `data-raw/ground-wide-query.txt` (≈2 × 1.8 km around the campus) → `npm run build:ground` → `public/data/ground.json` (≈1 MB, with `bbox` + `water`). The 4.5 MB raw extract is git-ignored; the curl line to refetch it is in `scripts/build-ground-data.mjs`. Neighbourhood buildings are always flat; only the campus is extruded in 3D. A ground plane covers the whole extract.
- **Cable network precomputed**: `npm run build:network` → `public/data/network.json` (routing took 1.2 s at startup).
- **Theme**: token-based (`index.css`), light / dark / system (`lib/theme.ts`, `<html data-theme>`), monospaced numerics, hairlines. Map palettes in `scene/minimal.ts` (light, dark, plus a night blend when the sun layer is on).
- **Map** (`scene/minimal.ts`): heat colours + glow, sun lighting and projected shadows from the real sun position, clouds from the irradiance station, cables with flow pulses, battery yard + substation (state / fault colours), PV panel rows (count follows roof coverage), trees, people, rooftop plant and floor slabs in 3D, pulsing rings on assets with active alarms. Animation (~24 fps) only while cables / clouds / alarm rings are visible.
- **Labels** are HTML anchored to buildings: hover card by default; *Top 8* / *All* pills in Settings. Legend folded into a button (pin it in Settings). Weather is a small chip, details on hover.
- **Settings** (`components/Settings.tsx`, gear in the header): appearance, map & layers (2D/3D, camera presets, rotate/tilt, every layer), time & sun (playback speed, dawn/noon/dusk/night, freeze sun), forecast, energy system (agent, batteries, solar per roof, panel tech — the old Solar/Battery tabs), faults & alarms, impact report (old Impact tab), equipment & budget (catalog with *Ask budget* + *Simulate*). Preferences persist in `localStorage`.
- **History / forecast** (`sim/forecast.ts`): "now" = same weekday + hour as the real clock (or fixed in Settings). Before now = measured; after = forecast demand (same weekday-hour profile of the previous 4 weeks, level-corrected) and solar (last 3 days), day-ahead prices for 24 h then repeated. The agent plans on the forecast. `derived.week` = shown series, `derived.actual` = measured.
- **Status** (`lib/mode.ts`, `components/ModeIndicator.tsx`): header pills LIVE / HISTORY / FORECAST (+ offset from now, *go live*) and REAL CAMPUS / SIMULATED (hover = list of changes, one-click reset). Map frame: green live, dashed blue forecast, violet simulated.
- **Calendar** (`components/Calendar.tsx`): month grid, any day with recorded data. **Timeline**: week / day zoom, 15 / 5-min snapping, ±1 h / ±15 min steps, NOW marker, hatched forecast, dashed forecast curve (optional measured overlay), alarm marks.
- **Faults & alarms** (`lib/alarms.ts`, `components/AlarmPanel.tsx`, bell in the header): broken battery units (removed from the fleet, agent re-plans), broken PV arrays, grid outage window (unserved energy estimate), over-demand, price spikes, high CO₂, low battery, telemetry gaps. Active / upcoming (predicted) / past; click jumps the timeline.
- **Cables follow the streets** (`scene/streetNetwork.ts`, run by `npm run build:network`): road graph from OSM, assets attached to the nearest street, shortest-path tree from the substation, chains merged and drawn 3 m beside the street; `edge.down` = buildings fed (sizes the conductor). Every cable is drawn in its flow colour with moving dots in the flow direction: density ~1 dot per 100 m per 120 kW (max 10); speed grows smoothly with power from 0.3 m/s (near idle) to ~12 m/s (busiest), from wall-clock time so it never jumps.
- **Asset sheets** (`data/assets.ts`): type, specification, age and condition for buildings, PV arrays, battery units, cable segments and the substation, with ageing (PV −0.5 %/yr, LFP SOH −2 %/yr, XLPE −1.2 %/yr). Years/specs are estimates and labelled so. *Apply equipment ageing* (Settings → Energy system) feeds SOH and PV degradation into the simulation (off by default to keep the challenge numbers).
- **Click anything** (building, PV array, battery yard, substation, cable): the map centres on it and the sidebar opens its panel (`BuildingPanel` / `AssetPanel`). Cables, the battery yard and the substation show a week **history** of the power through them (`components/FlowHistory.tsx`, cable series from `lib/cables.ts`): now / peak / average / energy each way / max loading vs rating, hourly chart (measured vs forecast, coloured by direction), energy by day, typical day.
- **Go live** (header pill and "Jump to now") also pauses playback (`goLive()` in the store).
- **Simulated equipment is visible**: added battery units get their own violet containers in the yard, roofs with changed solar get violet panels + outline, and a violet "sim" tag sits on each; the header's SIMULATED pill lists every change (new / changed / fault / setting) and each row flies to it.
- **Solar panels tab** (`components/SolarTab.tsx`): the building sidebar has *Overview* / *Solar panels*. Each installed panel row (4 modules, `panel:<osmId>:<n>`, label `PV-C4-07`) is listed with live output, health and inspection note (`panelSheet` in `data/assets.ts`, simulated per-panel ageing + findings). Hover a row → that panel is highlighted on the roof; click → panel sheet + map zooms to it. Clicking a panel on the map opens the same view.
- **Supply by source** (`lib/supply.ts`, `components/SupplyBar.tsx`): every demand view splits public grid (orange), battery (teal) and solar (amber), the same colours as the cables — building week chart (stacked), "supplied now" bars in the building panel, hover card and suggested-action card, and the timeline curve; hovering the timeline shows a per-hour tooltip (time, history/live/forecast, demand, price, % and kW from grid / battery / solar). Solar first, then battery discharge, the rest from the grid; buildings share the campus mix in proportion to their demand.
- **Stations**: larger compounds filled UPC blue (substation, 8 m tall in 3D) and teal (battery yard), red on faults, with a contrasting casing + darker edge outline (no icons); names, live figures and any simulated changes appear in the hover card only. Heat glow is off by default (Settings → Map & layers).
- **Cable fix**: street pieces not connected to the substation's network (e.g. around Library OB and FEE) are now bridged to the nearest reached street, so every building has a feed.
- **Sidebar** (toggle in the header): assistant chat, alarm list, or **building panel** (click a building: week demand history + forecast, peak / average / base load / load factor / intensity, energy by day, typical day, all 35 weeks, roof solar + fault toggle, alarms, "Ask the assistant").

## Known issues / limits
1. `src/lib/format.ts` and `src/lib/sun.ts` were **re-created from their call sites** (the originals were never committed). Sun position is the NOAA algorithm with Europe/Madrid DST applied to the stored local-naive clock; sanity-checked (solstice noon ≈ 72°, winter ≈ 24°) but not diffed against the original.
2. The in-app browser pane is software-rendered (screenshots time out, scripts run slowly). Layout was verified by measuring panel rectangles at the three widths plus screenshots of scenes; **look at the pitch once on a real GPU before the demo** (frame rate was not measured).
3. Insights sweeps use 7 spread weeks (`SWEEP_WEEKS`) to stay fast; "agent action by hour" uses all 35 weeks. Numbers differ slightly from all-week means.
4. Pitch scene durations assume the sweeps finished (pre-warmed). On a very slow machine the agent/horizon scenes may show a short "Running the real agent…" bar.
5. Pitch markings assume near-rectangular pitches (stripes follow the outline, lines use the bounding box). Water, richer roofs, building entrances are not done.
6. Side-by-side compare (split view) in the 3D scene: not done (the Impact tab has the numbers).
7. HMR of `useSim.ts` recreates the store: reload the page after editing it. Dev-only `window.__app` exposes `useSim`, `usePitch`, `SCENES`, `camera` for scripted checks.
8. Bundle > 500 kB (deck.gl): fine for a local demo. `npm run lint` (oxlint) only reports Fast-Refresh export warnings.

## How to refresh data after model changes
```bash
cd demo/frontend
<repo>/.venv/Scripts/python -I scripts/export_replay.py --repo <checkout that has data/train, sim/ and agent/>   # rewrites public/data/replay.json
npm run validate          # TS port must still match Python; if agent/agent.py changed, port the change to src/sim/challenge.ts first
npm run build
```
Node ≥ 22 is required (Vite 8, `--experimental-strip-types` for validate).
- **Substation 3D model** (`buildMinimal` → `M.substation.parts`, layer `substation-plant`): fenced gravel yard (gate on the street side), control building in the station colour (red on outage), two transformers on plinths with radiators, conservator tanks, HV/LV bushings and a fire wall, breaker/disconnector poles, three steel gantries carrying the busbars, and an incoming-line terminal pole. Drawn flat (top view) in 2D.
- **Battery yard 3D model** (`M.battery.parts` + 20 `containers` slots, layer `assets`): fenced gravel pad 42 x 30 m, containers on plinths with end-wall HVAC units and doors (roof = state colour: teal charging, orange discharging, green idle; violet simulated, red fault), empty plinths for unused slots; one container per ~400 kWh; when the 20 ground slots are full, extra containers stack on top (level 2, 3, ...), two power-conversion skids, step-up transformer, MV switchgear kiosk, cable trench. The pulsing alarm ring on the map was removed (alarms stay in the banner and the alarm panel).
- **Cable source breakdown** (`lib/cables.ts` `cableMixAt`, shown in `AssetPanel`): % grid / battery / solar of the power in a cable, now and over the week's energy. Estimate: feeders carry the network's hourly mix (grid import, battery discharge, rooftop solar other buildings push back); the battery's own cable = solar surplus then grid while charging, all battery while discharging.
- **Resizable sidebar** (`components/SideFrame.tsx`): drag its left edge (300 px to 70 % of the window, double-click resets to 380 px); width remembered in localStorage `cn.sideW`. Panels fill the frame (`w-full`).
- **Camera locked to the map** (`fitToBbox` in `MapView.tsx`): the four screen corners are projected to the ground (pitch/bearing aware); zoom-out and panning stop when they would leave the ground bbox, also on fly-tos and container resize. Max pitch now 60°.
- **Add / remove solar panels from a building** (`SolarTab.tsx`): "+ Add panel" / "−" in the Solar panels tab, with "n of N places used". Panel rows have a fixed rank, so one more panel = the coverage share set between the last shown row's rank and the next; the model's PV output follows the new coverage and the roof shows as simulated (violet).
